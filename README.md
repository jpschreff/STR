# STR Tax Strategy — Sierra Cash Flow Line

Working analysis for identifying a Bay-Area-drivable short-term rental that
qualifies for the OBBBA (One Big Beautiful Bill Act) 100% bonus depreciation
and the STR loophole, to meaningfully offset ~$1.6M/yr of K-1 income.

Full report (rendered): [analysis/sierra-cash-flow-line.html](analysis/sierra-cash-flow-line.html)

## Key findings

- **Bonus depreciation + STR loophole stack, but with real limits.** A cost
  segregation study can put ~25% of building basis into 5/7/15-year property,
  100% deductible in year one under OBBBA — but only losses that clear
  §469's material-participation and 7-day-average-stay tests become
  non-passive and offset W-2/K-1 income. **California does not conform** to
  federal bonus depreciation, so the ~$111–140K first-year tax benefit
  modeled here is a **federal-only** number.
- **Most classic Bay-Area weekend-house markets are now closed to a new STR
  buyer.** Town of Truckee (cap reached, 365-day resale wait), Douglas
  County NV/Zephyr Cove-Kingsbury (cap nearly full), and Sonoma
  County/Russian River (new permits effectively blocked by zoning + density
  caps) were all screened out.
- **Shortlist:** North Lake Tahoe (Placer Co., ~300 of 3,900 permits open),
  South Lake Tahoe (El Dorado Co., ~300 of 900 open, verify weekly), and
  Incline Village/Crystal Bay NV (parcel-specific eligibility, no hard cap).
- **Budget finding:** stretching to a $2.0M/20%-down purchase does not
  improve cash flow versus a $1.6M/25%-down purchase — the added debt
  service outruns the added revenue a bigger house commands. Incline
  Village's lower property tax gets closest to operating breakeven at the
  $1.6M tier.
- The bonus depreciation tax shield functions as the thing that converts a
  ~$10–35K/yr year-one operating shortfall into a net-positive year, not as
  a source of ongoing cash flow — property selection (permit headroom, ADR
  premium features, low ongoing opex) still drives years 2+.

This is a modeling framework, not tax/legal/investment advice or a live
listing search — verify all figures with a CPA licensed in CA/NV and a
local buyer's agent before writing an offer.
